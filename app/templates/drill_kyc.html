How these figures are defined

"Passed" is the Account created status (full-KYC Premium accounts). "Failed" is every verification-error status plus Rejected. "Outstanding" is any application still in review or approval. Abandoned applications are excluded from attempts. Lite accounts do not have their own status in the report, so this view describes the full-KYC application flow. Confirm these definitions with your compliance team before quoting the rates externally. They live in app/services/statuses.py.
